Design your app

Can someone see the whole population, which rows were sampled, and how the sample's estimate compares with the truth?

Learning objective
Design a display that makes a sample {{help sample}} visible inside its population, and compare a sample mean {{help sample-mean}} with the true mean {{help true-mean}}.
Reader

Try it yourself

Open the app and, without reading your code, point to: the full population, three sampled rows, the sample mean and the true mean. Change the seed: does it stay obvious which rows are sampled? Then write one sentence on what your chosen visual feature would show if a sample were badly unrepresentative.

Claims to avoid

  • “The sample mean is close to the true mean, so the method works.” One sample cannot show that.
  • An unlabelled line or number that could be either the sample mean or the true mean.

Common questions

Why a temporary random sample?

It lets you test the display before the three methods exist. Stage 3 replaces it with simple random, systematic and stratified sampling.

My agent suggested a design I like better than my own idea. Can I use it?

Yes. Choosing well is the skill. Record why it suits your reader and your data, and what it is meant to help them learn.

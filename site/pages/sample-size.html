Change sample size

How does sample size change the reliability of estimates, and what can a larger sample not fix?

Learning objective
Show, with repeated samples at several sizes, how the spread of estimates changes with n {{help sample-size}}, and tell that apart from a single sample's error.
Reader
The health professional deciding how large a sample to pay for.
Data
The baseline population, data/{{sc file}}, held fixed. Simple random sampling at n = 10, 40, 160 and 640 at least; one method at a time; at least 1,000 repetitions per size.

Try it yourself: explain a sample-size result

Find, in your app, one sample at a larger n whose estimate is farther from the true mean than some sample at a smaller n. Explain in two sentences why that does not contradict your repeated-sampling result. Then ask yourself: could someone using my app understand how sample size affects reliability?

Claims to avoid

  • “A bigger sample is always closer to the truth.” Larger samples are closer on average; any one sample can still miss.
  • “A larger sample fixes bias.” Think about what larger samples cannot repair.
  • Changing the method or the population at the same time as n, then crediting n for the difference.

Common questions

Why fix the method and the population?

To isolate one cause. If anything else changes along with n, you cannot tell which change produced the difference.

Can I compare other methods across sizes too?

Yes, as an extra, one method at a time, with the same repetitions per size. Check that every size you offer is valid for that method (for example, the systematic interval and the stratified allocation).

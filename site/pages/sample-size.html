More help

Extending to systematic or stratified sampling

Keep to one method at a time, label each size's set, and check that every size is valid for that method.

Systematic: use the whole-number interval when N/n is whole (exact enumeration is then available) and the fractional interval otherwise. Never drop a size because N/n is not whole. For your file:

{{intervals clinic}} {{intervals costs}} {{intervals heart}}

Stratified: n must be at least the number of strata (one row per stratum) and no larger than N.

Why fix the method and the population?

To isolate one cause. If anything else changes along with n, you cannot tell which change produced the difference.

Claims to avoid
  • “A bigger sample is always closer to the truth.” Larger samples are closer on average; any one sample can still miss.
  • “A larger sample fixes bias.” Think about what larger samples cannot repair.
  • Changing the method or the population at the same time as n, then crediting n for the difference.

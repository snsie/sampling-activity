Claims to avoid in your write-up
  • Generalising beyond your file: your population is the rows of your CSV.
  • A ranking of methods without the population, n, and measure (bias, SD or RMSE) it depends on.
  • Conclusions drawn from one sample, or from one seed, instead of repeated sampling.
